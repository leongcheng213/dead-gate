Press ` any time during the hunt to open the shop window — weapons, ammo, upgrades, stats, aid and extra life are all in there.
12 weapons: Pistol, Rifle, Shotgun, SMG, Sniper, Minigun, 💣 Grenade (hold CLICK to charge, release to lob), 🚀 Rocket, ⚫ Mine, 🔮 Plasma, ☠️ Bane, 🔱 Railgun (bolts pierce walls) — kills reveal them in the shop, cash buys your own copy (+ammo, single combined upgrade to Lv15, ✚ aid)
Zombies drop $ coins • buy stats (❤ max HP, 🛡 defense, 👟 speed), aid, 💖 life • upgrade each weapon with ONE button to Lv15 (damage + rate + mag every level, pierce/blast every 3) • ⚠️ explosives hurt YOU too • 👹 Boss every 10 rounds — Brute, Spitter, Blink, Wraith, Summoner, then combos! • new breeds join as rounds climb: spitters, dashers, bombers, leapers, speeders, splitters, menders, shades…
DIFFICULTY (pick above): 🟢 EASY baseline • 🟡 NORMAL tougher zombies, pricier shops, richer coins • 🔴 HARD brutal horde, best scores

👥 MULTIPLAYER up to 4 hunters • zombies get tougher

🤝 Teammate bullets can't hurt you — but 💣🚀 explosions can, watch your spacing!
🛒 The shop does not pause the hunt in co-op — buy carefully, zombies keep coming. ESC opens the menu (no pause) — leave the room there if needed.
💰 Coins are personal: whoever grabs one keeps it (drops grow ~1.5× with 2 hunters). HP carries between rounds — the fallen stay fallen, top up with E.
🔫 Arsenals are personal too: your unlocks, levels and ammo are yours alone — buys spend your own coins.
👻 Death leaves a 👻 ghost + red-arrow body: a mate buys 💉 Revive Kit, walks to the body and presses T. No self-rebirth here!
